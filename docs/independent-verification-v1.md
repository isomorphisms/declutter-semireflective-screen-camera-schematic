Central signatures:

```idric

```

`verify_coverage` inputs are original decoded photograph, assembled reference,
candidate readability view and selected-source map, in that order. Shared P6
parsing and checked RGB access are reused. Independent decisions do not import
production enhancement, aperture selection or registration functions.

Witnesses explicitly separate feature kind from `observed`, `ambiguous` and
`unobserved` evidence. Contamination cannot satisfy electrical acceptance.
Every required rectangle must retain its expected source ID and every original
reference sample at its independently declared native translation. Frozen RGB
probes authenticate the measured local samples. The CLI also checks the decoded
P6 SHA-256 using the host sha256sum boundary. All 12 original JPEG digests were
independently checked against source-manifest; layout and decoded hashes are
recorded in each manifest. JPEG/layout admission is recorded externally, rather
than falsely claimed as an in-module decoder or SHA implementation.

Each original mark/background pair must have channel difference at least 8.
The candidate must retain that direction and at least
`max(4, ceil(original_difference/2))` byte levels. This fixed requirement was
chosen before candidate execution; it is not a confidence score or a general
readability model. It checks particular observed strokes and adjacent spaces,
not OCR, every glyph, every trace pixel, or semantic equivalence of a whole
symbol. Sparse probes can miss other alterations. Complete reference rectangles
plus sparse rendered probes are the stated boundary; local topology remains a
separate obligation.

At least one observed wire segment and one marking, with probes on every required
witness, are required at both manifest admission and the exported verifier.
Zero witnesses, all-unresolved evidence, all-grey candidate data and all-unknown
maps cannot grant photographic PASS. Bounds: 64 KiB manifest, 64 witnesses,
256 probes, 4096 pixels per witness, admitted image/coordinate limits. RGB is
contiguous; no per-pixel linked lists or host Double. Direct optical sums use
Integer; bounded raster addresses use the existing checked implementation.
